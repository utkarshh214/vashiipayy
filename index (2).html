Q1: "Is this real?"

This is a working local prototype with a real logistic regression scoring pipeline, two deterministic hard rules, and a stateful ledger, connected to a simulated bank gateway rather than live NPCI or bank rails.

Q2: "Why synthetic data?"

Real UPI transaction logs and fraud labels are strictly confidential bank data. We trained our logistic regression on 20,000 synthetic transactions (ROC AUC 0.81) with hand-written rule labels to demonstrate the end-to-end scoring and pre-PIN intervention pipeline.

Q3: "How do you avoid blocking legitimate payments?"

Instead of silently dropping borderline payments, Vashi Pay introduces pre-PIN cognitive friction—a Hindi/English voice warning, a Reversal Cognitive Mirror showing money is leaving the account, and a mandatory confirmation phrase (I AM SENDING MONEY) so legitimate users can still proceed.

Q4: "What would it take to connect to a bank or NPCI?"

Integrating the scoring hook into the PSP app's pre-PIN checkout screen, pulling live payee VPA creation timestamps and account-name match flags from the NPCI/PSP switch, and retraining the model on historical bank dispute logs.

Known Limits to Mention if Asked

  • What-If Playground vs Server Scoring: The What-If controls use the browser's copy of the model for immediate feedback, so their numbers can differ slightly from the server's.
  • Rolling Velocity Window: The velocity rule counts every payment in five minutes, so the rapid test depends on what ran before it (use Reset DB before running Rapid payments).
